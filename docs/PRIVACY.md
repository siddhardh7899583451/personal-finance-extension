# Privacy Policy

**Personal Finance & Expense Intelligence** is a local-first Chrome extension.

## What we collect

**Nothing remotely.**

- No user accounts
- No servers
- No advertising identifiers
- No browsing history access
- No financial data transmission

All transactions, budgets, goals, and settings remain in your browser’s local storage (IndexedDB and `chrome.storage.local`).

## Exports

When you export JSON or CSV, files are generated on your device and downloaded through the browser. You control where they go.

## Permissions

| Permission | Reason |
|------------|--------|
| `storage` | Save your financial data locally |
| `alarms` | Optional local reminders for recurring items (future) |

No `tabs` host access beyond opening the extension’s own dashboard page.

## Contact

This is an open project. For privacy questions, open an issue on the repository.
